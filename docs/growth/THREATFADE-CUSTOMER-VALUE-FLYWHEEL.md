# ThreatFade Customer Value Flywheel

**Status:** Strategic baseline  
**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Scope:** Customer onboarding, time-to-value, adoption, expansion, advocacy, and retention  

## Purpose

Turn every successful ThreatFade deployment into deeper customer value, expansion revenue, credible references, and new product/research inputs.

## Flywheel

```text
Customer deployment
      ↓
Time-to-first-detection
      ↓
Evidence reviewed
      ↓
Operational workflow
      ↓
More use cases / environments
      ↓
Expansion
      ↓
Customer success
      ↓
Reference / case study / research
      ↓
New qualified demand
      ↺
```

## Customer value milestones

### M0 — Deployment

ThreatFade is securely deployed in the agreed environment.

### M1 — First successful detection

The customer produces a valid, reviewable result.

### M2 — Analyst adoption

Analysts repeatedly use the evidence/investigation workflow.

### M3 — Operational integration

ThreatFade connects to the customer's workflow, SIEM/SOAR, evidence process, or detection engineering practice.

### M4 — Measurable value

The customer can articulate a validated detection, workflow, coverage, efficiency, or research benefit.

### M5 — Expansion

Additional environments, use cases, integrations, detection packs, or services are adopted.

### M6 — Advocacy

Customer agrees to a reference, testimonial, case study, benchmark, conference contribution, or research collaboration where appropriate.

## Onboarding principle

Do not optimize for feature activation. Optimize for:

> **time from secure deployment to the first meaningful evidence-backed detection.**

## Customer success plan

Every Enterprise/Pilot engagement should have:

- named owner;
- deployment scope;
- baseline;
- success criteria;
- target detection scenarios;
- data sources;
- integration plan;
- training plan;
- review cadence;
- expansion hypotheses;
- renewal decision timeline.

## Expansion paths

### Environment expansion

One environment → multiple environments → business units → regions.

### Capability expansion

Core detection → advanced packs → integrations → automation → managed detection.

### Deployment expansion

Cloud → private cloud → hybrid → on-premises where justified.

### Service expansion

Platform → implementation → custom detection → managed operation → research.

## Expansion signals

Look for:

- new telemetry sources;
- increased analyst usage;
- new business units requesting access;
- repeated detection requests;
- additional integration requests;
- new compliance requirements;
- increased deployment scope;
- requests for custom detection;
- requests for managed operations.

Expansion should follow demonstrated value rather than artificial feature gating.

## Customer evidence loop

With explicit permission and appropriate anonymization, customer outcomes can become:

- case studies;
- benchmarks;
- research questions;
- technical articles;
- detection-pack improvements;
- conference material;
- product roadmap inputs.

Never expose customer-sensitive data or imply a customer result beyond what was actually measured.

## Health metrics

Track:

- time-to-first-detection;
- time-to-first-value;
- weekly/monthly active analysts;
- repeat investigations;
- environments covered;
- detection-pack adoption;
- integration adoption;
- expansion ARR;
- renewal rate;
- net revenue retention;
- support burden;
- customer-reported value;
- referenceability.

## Customer health model

### Green

Regular usage + measurable value + expanding scope.

### Yellow

Usage declining, unresolved technical friction, or unclear value.

### Red

No meaningful usage, blocked deployment, unresolved trust/security issue, or no owner.

Interventions should be evidence-led and proportionate.

## Advocacy mechanism

Do not ask for a testimonial immediately after purchase.

Ask after a measurable outcome:

**Deployment → result → review → value confirmation → reference request.**

## Definition of a healthy customer-value flywheel

Customers reach value quickly, adopt repeatedly, expand when additional value is proven, renew because ThreatFade is operationally useful, and—where permitted—produce evidence that helps future customers understand the product.

## Relationship to other flywheels

**Commercial → Customer Value → Expansion / Advocacy → Content / Research → Demand → Conversion.**
