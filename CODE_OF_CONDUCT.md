# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the ThreatFade community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project.
- Showing courtesy and respect in community interactions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issue content, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces. Examples include using an official project email address, posting through an official social account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **security@tinlance.com**, marking the subject **ThreatFade Web — Code of Conduct report**. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. The maintainers will acknowledge reports as promptly as practical and handle them confidentially to the extent possible.

For security vulnerabilities, use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the nature, context, and impact of each incident and may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. **Correction:** A private or public reminder of the community standards.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning and explanation that continued behavior is unacceptable.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A temporary restriction from project spaces or interactions.
4. **Permanent restriction:** A permanent ban from project spaces.

Maintainers may skip steps when the severity of an incident warrants it.
